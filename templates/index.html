How It Works

1

Admin Registration

University admin registers the institution and configures the complete timetable with courses, faculty assignments, and class schedules.

2

Faculty Marks Attendance

Faculty conducts lectures according to timetable and marks student attendance in real-time through the system interface.

3

Records & Reports

Attendance data is securely stored in the database. Generate comprehensive reports and track student attendance patterns.

Core Features

Timetable-Based Control

Attendance is synchronized with institutional timetable ensuring lectures are tracked according to schedule.

Role-Based Access

Separate access levels for Admin, Faculty, and Students with appropriate permissions and capabilities.

Proxy Prevention

Built-in logic to prevent proxy attendance and ensure accurate attendance marking.

Attendance Records

Complete digital records of attendance stored securely in centralized database for easy access.

Reports & Analytics

Generate detailed attendance reports with analytics for students, courses, and time periods.

Alerts Future Scope

Planned feature for automated notifications to students and parents regarding attendance status.
